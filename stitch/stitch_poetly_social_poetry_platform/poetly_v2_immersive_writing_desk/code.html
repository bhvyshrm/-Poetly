Writing to Prompt: "A place you never truly left"
Prompt Seeded

We left the lantern burning at the rim of the orchard,
believing light was something
a wandering fox or traveler could borrow
without diminishing its heart.

✦ · · ✦

Now the frost arrives in quiet syllables,
coating the brambles in salt-white glass.
Whatever you could not speak in daylight
settles here, between the breathing of the wood.

A breath drawn inward, holding the perimeter—
where every window remembers the silhouette
of what was promised before the thaw.
