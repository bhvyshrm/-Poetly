Elena Rostova

@elena.r

2h ago · Prague

The Geography of Longing

We leave rooms as if we might return within the hour,
leaving tea leaves suspended in lukewarm water,
the book cracked open at the spine.

Yet years drift across the threshold,
heavy as uncombed wool,
quiet as dust finding its familiar chair.

A map is only paper until you trace
the cold distance between two closed doors.

#nocturne #solitude #memory

Marcus Thorne

@mthorne

4h ago · Cornwall

After the Salt Tide

The wet slate remembers nothing of the storm,
only that water came, heavy and deliberate,
erasing the small calligraphy of shorebirds.

Here, where the gray channel widens toward Ireland,
even grief turns luminous under the breaking clouds.

#oceanic #grief #transience
