Cartography of Fog

We drew the harbor entirely from memory,
forgetting where the wet jetty ended and the low clouds began.
No buoy answered our ringing bell.

Only the tide, folding upon its own cold hem,
whispering that distance is not an absence of earth—
merely a quiet waiting for lanterns to breathe.
